# 🍽️ Dishcovery
Exploring the Indian Culinary Spectrum
What began as a painful trip to the dentist— three teeth lighter and a lecture about my "eating habits" quickly turned into a meaningful data exploration. Faced with project deadlines and limited time, I turned to something close to home: Indian cuisine.

🎯 Project Inspiration
As I recovered, I started thinking about how food shapes our lives and how eating habits vary widely across Indian states. My love for sweets? Not entirely my fault! A quick look at food data showed that Andhra Pradesh is one of the states known for its sweet palate. That revelation sparked the idea: could I tell a story about Indian food using data?
🔍 This project can also be helpful for entrepreneurs or restaurateurs planning to open restaurants in specific states, by providing insights into local tastes, preferences, and cooking trends.

🛠️ Tools & Techniques
To bring this idea to life, I used:
Python for data cleaning and preprocessing
Basic NLP techniques (tokenization, frequency analysis) to analyze ingredient lists and recipe steps
Data visualization libraries like Plotly and Seaborn
Interactive maps to showcase regional culinary trends GeoJSON

📊 What I Explored
Ingredient Distribution: Which ingredients are common across states, and which are unique?
Cooking Time Analysis: How do preparation and cooking times differ by region or dish type?
Cultural Patterns: Clustering dishes by spice levels, sweetness, or vegetarian status
Regional Specialties: Highlighting what makes each state's cuisine distinct

🧠 Key Takeaways
This wasn’t a large-scale, enterprise-level project—but it didn’t need to be. It showed me how even simple, personally inspired projects can lead to powerful insights. More importantly, it taught me to connect data work to human experiences, making the results more relatable and meaningful.

**If data can make you smile through a toothache, imagine what it can do at scale.**
